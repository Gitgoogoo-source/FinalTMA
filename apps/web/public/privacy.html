EvoMyPet Telegram Mini App

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EvoMyPet Team (“EvoMyPet”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the EvoMyPet Telegram bot and Mini App.

Information we handle

  • Telegram account information supplied through Telegram, including your Telegram user ID, username, first name, last name and language. We do not store your Telegram profile photo URL.
  • Game information such as balances, pets, collections, progress, tasks, referrals, marketplace activity and battle activity.
  • Purchase and payment records needed to create, verify, deliver, reconcile and support Telegram Stars purchases. We do not receive your card or app-store payment credentials.
  • Limited technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service, such as request identifiers, timestamps, error codes and anti-abuse records.
  • The verified Telegram launch source for your first registration and later sessions, such as a direct entry, referral, directory listing or advertising campaign link. Campaign reports use aggregated user, activity and purchase counts.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate your Telegram account, operate the game, save progress, complete purchases and rewards, enable social and multiplayer features, measure how directory and advertising links bring and retain users, prevent abuse, resolve payment issues and keep the service reliable.

Service providers

EvoMyPet relies on Telegram and infrastructure providers for hosting, data storage and realtime delivery. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and security

We retain account, game and transaction records for as long as they are needed to operate EvoMyPet, protect users, meet platform or legal obligations and resolve disputes. We use access controls, signed Telegram authentication, short-lived sessions and server-side authorization to protect service data. No online service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can stop using EvoMyPet at any time. The Mini App currently does not provide a self-service account deletion control. To ask about your information or make a request available under applicable law, contact us through Telegram at @EvoMyPetSupport.

Children

EvoMyPet is not directed to children who are below the minimum age required to use Telegram or digital purchase features in their location. Do not use EvoMyPet if you are not legally permitted to do so.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
