# Monster Tamer Asset Provenance

Monster Tamer no longer ships the project-original monster portraits, trainer
illustrations, battle background, capture items, party UI, inventory UI,
encounter tiles, or battle assets used by the retired exploration game.

The current collection home uses exactly two visual sources:

1. FinalTMA Catalog v1's existing official collection thumbnails and detail
   images. They remain owned and generated by the main catalog asset pipeline;
   Monster Tamer references their versioned public paths and does not copy or
   transform them.
2. The audited 32-file Tiny Swords Free Pack selection recorded in
   `assets/licenses/tiny-swords/SOURCE.json` and `TERMS.md`.

`tools/monster-tamer/generate-island-map.mjs` authors the 50×50 island layout,
the uninterrupted two-tile water ring, connected walkable mask, collision
footprints, scenery placement, and compact terrain atlas. No character,
animal, resource, tool, Particle FX, UI, cloud, rubber duck, Aseprite,
alternate faction, or Enemy Pack asset is used.

The retained favicon predates this replacement and is used only as the static
renderer document icon.
